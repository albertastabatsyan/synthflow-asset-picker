Synthflow Asset Picker

A shared gallery for vibe-curating Synthflow's Figma asset estate. We sweep the libraries together, pool the good pieces in one shared Vibe Bucket, and take the result away as a moodboard.

What you're looking at

Every visual from the Social Media & Ads and Marketing (Landing Pages & Graphics) Figma files — switch libraries with the dropdown at the top of the left sidebar. Near-duplicates are stacked into one card (the ×N corner tag); the page list below mirrors the Figma file's pages. The Custom library is the shared home for pieces that live outside Figma — ⬆ upload or drag & drop screenshots, exports and references there (everyone sees them; remove your own with ✕) and pick them like any other asset.

Picking

Click the info strip under any thumbnail to pick the asset into the shared bucket — click again to unpick; everyone sees it within seconds, tagged with your name. The "pick into" dropdown sets the category your picks land in (optional). Pick the same thing as a teammate and it gets a ×2 consensus badge and floats up. Click the thumbnail (or press Enter) to zoom: arrow keys flip through assets (←→) and near-dupes (↑↓), number keys 1-9 0 Q W E pick straight into a category, clicking the image closes. In the grid, arrows move in all four directions; ← on the leftmost column jumps to the page list (↑↓ flip through pages — the grid follows live — and → dives in). Space picks the highlighted card and hide picked keeps your sweep clean.

The Vibe Bucket — from candidates to finals

The shared pool, and the funnel from the raw libraries to ready-to-use assets. The category row on top is shared and editable — ＋ adds, ✎ renames, ✕ deletes a category (its picks move to Uncategorized) — and dragging any card onto a category re-files it for everyone; clicking a category jumps to its section. Picks from unambiguous library pages file themselves into the matching category when you pick (re-file any time); ▶ Sort unfiled zooms through the rest — a category key files each one and advances. Every pick is a candidate: press ☆ to promote the keepers to ★ final, and ★ Final only turns the bucket into the shelf of ready-to-use assets. Remove your own picks any time; ✕ all clears an item for everyone (it asks first); re-categorize in zoom. Each card's ⬇ downloads that asset at the highest available quality — uploads at original quality, library assets at the highest live Figma export scale that succeeds (otherwise the gallery render). When the pool is ready, ⬇ Download moodboard produces a single file with everything — images, names, credits, Figma links — and Copy Figma links gives the plain-text list.

Good to know

Every card's Figma ↗ jumps to the real asset in Figma (you need access to that Figma file). Nothing here writes to Figma — picking is opinion-pooling, not moving files. Your name and picks are visible to everyone with the link. The name is remembered in this browser — you're asked once per device; click the picking as … chip up top to change it any time. (In-app browsers — e.g. opening the link inside Slack — may forget it; open in your regular browser instead.)
