How it works
Library is the raw Figma estate plus uploads, opening on the judging queue: every asset awaits one verdict — Pick it into Review (Space) or Reject it out of the queue (X). Rejections are shared with the team and reversible under the Rejected filter. Or open a need on Ready and gather a shortlist for it.
Review holds candidates. Publish one to give it a need, a clear title and an optional usage note.
Ready is the destination: published assets organized by the concrete need they serve, with coverage per required format. Use one via Open in Figma.
